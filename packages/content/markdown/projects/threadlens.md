ThreadLens is a self-hosted research tool for builders who want to find product opportunities in public conversations. It scouts Reddit, Bluesky, and Google Search, scores posts and results for pain and relevance, and generates reports that cluster findings into themes and product angles.

It runs locally with Docker or pnpm, stores data in SQLite, and keeps provider keys under your control.
